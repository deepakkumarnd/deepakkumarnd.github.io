About Me

Hi, I’m Deepak 👋

I’ve been writing software since 2011, and Ruby has been my programming language of choice for as long as I can remember. I enjoy building things, exploring new ideas, and thinking about how technology can solve interesting problems.

I live in Kochi, Kerala 🌴 with my wife Amrutha, and our two daughters, Daksha and Dyuthi.

This blog is my little corner of the internet where I write about software, technology, things I’m curious about, and the occasional thought that refuses to leave my head. If you enjoy exploring ideas, learning something new, or simply seeing how someone else thinks, welcome aboard!
